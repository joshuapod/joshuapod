# Joshua Poddoku

Contributor pipelines and governance for Open Source.

I work on the part of a project that isn't code: how someone finds a way in,
who decides what, and what happens when a maintainer steps away. Projects
rarely lose people over the code. They lose them because there is no clear way
in, and nobody has the hours to answer when someone tries.

I've done this for seven years, at a pre-seed startup, then a Series A devtool
company, then an agent company. At the devtool company the DevLake community
grew from 100 to over 400 engineering leaders across more than 100 enterprise
teams, and the project went from no stars to over 3,000.

## Ask me something

**[Open an issue here](https://github.com/joshuapod/joshuapod/issues/new)** and
say what your project is stuck on. Or open one on your own repo and tag
**@joshuapod**.

I will respond publicly, so the next person with the same problem can read it
too. I take on a few each month.

Good things to ask about:

- nobody outside the team is contributing, and the code is fine
- a `CONTRIBUTING`, `SECURITY` or code of conduct you haven't written yet
- what to say about AI-generated pull requests before they arrive
- roles, credit, and who decides once there's more than one maintainer
- a good first issue that nobody picks up
