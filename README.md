
# Joshua Poddoku

Signal over noise for Open Source ecosystems.

I work with people growing and maintaining an Open Source project, from the
repo to the ecosystem around it. It's consulting, plus a system that reads what
arrives: pull requests, issues, discussions, mentions, calls for papers. You
see what needs a decision, and nothing goes out without your approval.

Some projects I talk to lose people after their first pull request. Others have
more AI-written pull requests than anyone can review. Both are a signal
problem.

I've done this for seven years, at pre-seed to Series A startups that depended on Open Source.

## Ask me something

**[Open an issue here](https://github.com/joshuapod/joshuapod/issues/new)** and
say what your project is stuck on. Or open one on your own repo and tag
**@joshuapod**.

I will respond publicly, so the next person with the same problem can read it
too. I take on a few each month.

Good things to ask about:

- people contribute once and don't come back
- more pull requests than you can review, many of them AI-written
- a `CONTRIBUTING`, `SECURITY` or code of conduct you haven't written yet
- roles, credit, and who decides once there's more than one maintainer
- what people say about your project outside the repo, and what needs an answer
- which conferences and calls for papers are worth your time
